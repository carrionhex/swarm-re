How it works

Three moves. The swarm does the rest.

01

Stage the specimen

Drop any binary — EXE, DLL, ELF, APK — into the console or straight over curl. Nothing to install, no account, 16MB cap.

02

The swarm works

Mapper, identity, strings, tracer, and the deep engines check in live. The Master cross-examines every specialist claim before it counts.

03

Verdict card

Plain-English verdict: what the file is, what it does, what it hides — every statement backed by a replayable evidence bundle.
